# Hardware Design (Conceptual)

Suggested partitions: high-level AI compute; isolated real-time motion computer; vendor-supported joint/hand/head controllers; independent safety-rated relay/controller with physical E-stop and drive STO; encoders, IMU, foot force/contact sensors, depth/range cameras, temperature and power telemetry.

Select components against torque-speed envelopes, peak/continuous current, thermal limits, regenerative energy, encoder precision, bus timing, structural loading and serviceability. Battery chemistry, fusing, wire gauge, connector ratings, grounding, EMC and cooling require qualified design review.

No purchase-ready BOM or exact part ratings are established. Example position/speed bounds in code are placeholders, not safe physical limits.
